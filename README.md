## Smart Urban Mobility and Ride Management Platform

> **Move fast. Live more.**

SPEEDO is a modern urban mobility and ride-management platform prototype designed to provide a seamless digital experience for **customers, captains, and administrators**.

This is an **individual project** focused on designing and developing a professional frontend experience for a smart ride-booking platform.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Urban ride-booking services can face challenges such as:

* Uncertain pickup and waiting times
* Difficulty selecting suitable ride options
* Limited ride-status visibility
* Captain demand and earnings management challenges
* Limited operational insights
* Lack of centralized business analytics

SPEEDO aims to address these challenges through a unified digital mobility platform.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

SPEEDO provides dedicated interfaces for three major roles:

### 👤 Customer

* Ride booking
* Pickup and destination selection
* Bike / Auto / Cab selection
* Fare estimation
* Ride status
* Ride history

### 🏍️ Captain

* Online / Offline status
* Ride requests
* Pickup and destination information
* Ride management
* Earnings overview

### 🛡️ Admin

* Platform overview
* Customer monitoring
* Captain monitoring
* Ride activity
* Revenue insights
* Operational analytics
